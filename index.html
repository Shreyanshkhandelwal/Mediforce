1. Introduction

Welcome to Mediforce Health ("Mediforce", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of individuals who use our mobile application and related services.

Mediforce Health is a healthcare workforce management platform designed to support hospital workforce operations, employee management, shift applications, scheduling, attendance tracking, timesheets and payment-related records.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, use, disclose and protect information when you access or use our application, website or services.

By using Mediforce Health, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application and the features enabled by your hospital or employer, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Personal and Account Information

  • Full name and contact details.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Account login credentials and authentication information.
  • Employee or professional identification details.
  • Profile information provided during registration.

B. Employment and Workforce Information

  • Hospital, employer or organisation details.
  • Department, designation and professional role.
  • Shift applications and assignment information.
  • Shift schedules, availability and booking status.
  • Attendance, clock-in and clock-out records.
  • Timesheets, working hours and approval status.

C. Payment and Invoice Information

  • Recorded working hours and payment-related records.
  • Payment status and payment history.
  • Invoice details and associated transaction references.
  • Information required to administer payment records.

Where payment services are provided by an external payment provider, their collection and processing of payment information may also be governed by their own privacy policies.

D. Technical and Device Information

  • Device type, operating system and application version.
  • IP address and general device information.
  • Application usage and diagnostic information.
  • Crash reports and technical error information.

3. Location Information

Mediforce Health may use your device's location information to support attendance and workforce management functionality.

When location access is enabled and required for attendance, the application may collect GPS-based location information during clock-in and clock-out activities.

Location information may be used to:

  • Record the location associated with attendance events.
  • Support verification of attendance at a hospital or work site.
  • Help authorised employer representatives review attendance records.
  • Maintain accurate workforce and timesheet records.

Mediforce Health uses location information for attendance-related purposes. Location access should be governed by your device permissions and the location functionality enabled in the application.

You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may affect attendance features that require GPS verification.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide employee and workforce management services.
  • Facilitate shift applications, scheduling and assignments.
  • Record attendance and working hours.
  • Manage timesheets and approval workflows.
  • Maintain payment records and invoice information.
  • Send relevant application notifications and updates.
  • Respond to support requests and user enquiries.
  • Maintain application performance and security.
  • Prevent unauthorised access, misuse and fraudulent activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information in the following circumstances:

A. Hospitals and Employers

Information relevant to your employment and workforce activities may be made available to authorised hospital administrators, employers, coordinators, managers and other designated personnel.

This may include employee profiles, shift applications, assignments, attendance records, timesheets and payment-related records, as required for workforce administration.

B. Service Providers

We may share information with service providers that support the operation of our platform, such as hosting, database infrastructure, authentication, communications, analytics or payment services, where applicable.

C. Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where required by law, regulation, court order or a valid request from an authorised government authority.

D. Business Transfers

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of business assets, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

6. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction.

These measures may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, secure communications, infrastructure safeguards and operational security procedures, depending on the services deployed.

Access to employment and workforce information should be limited to authorised individuals who require it for legitimate operational purposes.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, administer employment-related records, fulfil legitimate operational purposes and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Attendance, timesheet, payment and invoice records may need to be retained for relevant administrative, financial, employment or legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable retention obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to your personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Requesting deletion of information where legally permitted.
  • Requesting information about how your data is processed.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raising concerns or submitting a privacy complaint.

Some employment and financial records may need to be retained to meet legal or contractual obligations. Certain requests may also need to be coordinated with the hospital or employer responsible for the relevant employment records.

To exercise your privacy rights, contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section.

9. Application Permissions

Mediforce Health may request access to certain device features to provide its functionality. Depending on the features enabled, these may include:

  • Location: To support GPS-based attendance recording.
  • Notifications: To send shift, assignment and account updates.
  • Camera: Only if a feature requires capturing or uploading images.
  • Photos or Files: Only if a feature requires selecting or uploading documents or images.

You can manage application permissions through your device settings. Some features may not work correctly if their required permissions are disabled.
